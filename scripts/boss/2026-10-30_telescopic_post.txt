Rear telescopic post failure is one of the most common problems on car haulers!

These posts often break near the lower bottom pin or at the upper sleeve when reinforcement is missing or worn out.

The locking mechanism must fit tightly against the pin-hole rail, with the locking pin entering the holes straight and perpendicular.

Worn holes, loose sleeves, and damaged locks allow movement during transport, potentially causing structural failure and damaging vehicles below.
