=== cracks | CRACKS IN THE STEEL | STRUCTURAL WELDING | Fields of space
You see a hairline crack at a bracket and tell yourself it can wait one more load. It can't. On a car carrier every crack sits in a part that carries weight, and the next load makes it longer. At Car Hauler Repair Houston we cut out fatigued steel, fabricate replacement sections, and weld them in where the stress actually lives. [HOOK 2] So why does a crack come back after a quick patch? Because a bead over a tired joint hides the problem instead of solving it. We reinforce high-stress areas with properly fitted structural plates, gussets and extra supports, so the repair carries load the way the original steel did. Welding is done right here in our bay, and finished repairs carry our six-month workmanship warranty. Found a crack? Call before your next load.
