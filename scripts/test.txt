=== rustrepair | CUT, WELD, COAT | REAL REPAIR, START TO FINISH | Thunder
Is your car hauler hiding rust you haven't looked at yet? Look at this ramp end: a worn latch, a rusted lip, a dirty perforated deck. The frame rail has cracked coating flaking off, with rust showing along the seam. This pivot clip is pitted, and the corroded steel is right there under your fingers. [HOOK 2] So what does it take to fix it right? First, the trailer is down to clean bare steel. The bad section is cut out. A steel plate sleeve goes in, and fresh welds go down at every joint. The deck plates get weld strips along the seams. Then the frame rails get a wet, glossy black coating, and the red brace is painted over a clean weld, with no visible rust. Welding is done on site, and completed repairs carry a six-month warranty on labor and workmanship. Bring it by the shop.
