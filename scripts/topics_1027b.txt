=== rampwidth | RAMPS TOO NARROW | FRONT EXTENSION WIDENING | Symphony one
Vehicles sitting too tight on the front extension ramps? That's a fitment problem, and it's fixable. We welded additional steel plates to the outer edges of the ramps, widening them to seventeen and three sixteenths inches. [HOOK 2] So why does width matter? Because a car that sits too tight is a car you can't load safely. The new extensions were ground smooth and finished in protective black paint. More width, proper fitment, safe loading.
