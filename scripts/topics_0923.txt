1) key: hydraulics — "HYDRAULICS" / "CYLINDERS, LINES & PUMPS" — music: Kama Sutra
========================================================================
Almost every moving part on a car hauler runs on hydraulics, and when the system starts to fail, loading turns into a fight. At Car Hauler Repair Houston we repair and replace hydraulic cylinders, hoses, fittings, valves, pumps, electric motors, reservoirs and PTO systems. A cylinder that still has good steel can be rebuilt with new seals and components, and the ones that are past saving get replaced. We trace leaks back to the actual source, check the lines and the mounting points, and test the deck through its full travel before the trailer leaves. Hydraulic problems rarely fix themselves, and they get expensive when a deck drops or sticks. Hydraulics acting up? Bring the trailer to Car Hauler Repair Houston.

3) key: corrosion — "CORROSION" / "CUT IT OUT, BUILD IT BACK" — music: Fields of space
========================================================================
Rust does not stop on its own. It works into seams, under skins and along the bottom rails, and by the time it shows on the outside, the steel underneath is usually thinner than it looks. At Car Hauler Repair Houston we cut out fatigued and damaged steel, fabricate replacement sections, and weld them back into the structure so the trailer carries load the way it was built to. Panels, rails, posts and skins all get treated the same way: remove what has failed, replace it with new material, and protect the repair against corrosion. Patching over rust only hides it for a season. Fighting rust on your trailer? Car Hauler Repair Houston can take care of it.

4) key: welding — "STRUCTURAL WELDING" / "WHERE THE LOAD GOES" — music: Thunder
========================================================================
Every load a car hauler carries ends up in its welds. At Car Hauler Repair Houston we repair cracked and damaged frame rails, crossmembers, side beams and lower frames, and we reinforce high-stress areas with properly fitted structural plates, gussets and additional supports. Each repair starts with cleaning the steel and fitting the new piece tight, because a weld is only as good as what sits underneath it. We weld on the trailer and on the bench, wherever the repair is best done, and the finished joints go back into service carrying the same load as the original structure. Welds are not the place to save money. Got a cracked frame or a broken bracket? Call Car Hauler Repair Houston.

9) key: whyblast — "WHY BLASTING" / "THE SURFACE DECIDES" — music: Another Day
========================================================================
People ask why we blast a trailer instead of just painting over it. This is why. Old coatings and rust hold moisture against the steel, so anything sprayed on top starts failing from underneath on day one. Blasting takes the frame back to clean bare metal, which is the only surface a primer can really bond to, and it also shows us the truth about the steel: where it is solid and where it needs repair before any coating goes on. It is slow, it is dirty, and it is the reason the finish still looks right years later. Thinking about paint for your hauler? Start the conversation with Car Hauler Repair Houston.
