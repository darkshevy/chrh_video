=== upperdeck | CRACK IN THE SUPPORT | STRUCTURAL DECK REPAIR | Another Day
A crack running through the main upper deck support. That's the structure the whole top deck rests on. We welded it closed, added a reinforcing plate, and coated the joint against corrosion. [HOOK 2] But why stop there? Steel crossmembers went in across the upper deck to spread the load, and the cracked aluminum ramp sections were welded back to full strength. Car Hauler Repair Houston.
