=== slideoutboss | SLIDE-OUT RAMP REPAIR | HOUSING, LOCK, RAMP DOOR | Another Day
See the rear ramp that slides out of its housing? It only works if three things hold: the housing, the lock, and the ramp door itself. On this car hauler the locking mechanism was damaged, and a damaged lock means the ramp can move when it should stay put. [HOOK 2] So why fix only the lock when the door can bend too? At Car Hauler Repair Houston we restored the rear slide-out ramp housing and replaced the damaged locking mechanism. Then we reinforced the rear ramp door with heavier angle steel, so it does not bend under load. Ramp door flexing when you load? Have it checked before it bends for good.

=== sleeveboss | TELESCOPIC POST REPAIR | SLEEVE AND LOCK RESTORED | Hollywood waves
Rear telescopic posts are one of the most common failures on car haulers. They usually break near the lower pin or at the upper sleeve, where the reinforcement is missing or worn out. [HOOK 2] So how do you spot trouble before it breaks? Look at the lock. It should sit tight against the pin-hole rail, with the pin going into the holes straight. Worn holes, a loose sleeve or a damaged lock let the post move on the road, and that can lead to structural failure and damaged vehicles below. On this trailer, Car Hauler Repair Houston replaced the damaged sleeve and spring lock assembly and set it back to a tight, straight fit. See play in your posts? Call the number on screen.
