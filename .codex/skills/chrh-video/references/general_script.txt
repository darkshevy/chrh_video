At Car Hauler Repair Houston, we specialize exclusively in car hauler repair, structural restoration, fabrication, hydraulics, and complete trailer rebuilding. Whether you need a small repair or a complete reconstruction, our shop is equipped to handle the entire project from beginning to end.

We perform major structural repairs including cracked and damaged main frame rails, tandem sections, front frame and kingpin areas, crossmembers, side beams, lower frames, telescopic posts, overhead racks, guide tracks, ramps, and hydraulic pull-out decks. When a frame loses its geometry after an accident or years of heavy use, we can straighten and realign the structure using hydraulic pulling equipment, remove fatigued or damaged steel, fabricate replacement sections, and reinforce high-stress areas with properly fitted structural plates, gussets, and additional supports.

Our fabrication department works with steel, aluminum, and stainless steel. We replace damaged beams and crossmembers, fabricate custom brackets and structural components, rebuild ramp systems, extend or modify trailer structures, and perform custom upgrades for specific hauling requirements.

We also specialize in complete decking replacement. That includes removal of old decking, rebuilding the support framework, replacing crossmembers, repairing upper and lower decks, installing steel or aluminum decking, rebuilding rear decks, hydraulic pull-out sections, and loading ramps. For projects requiring additional strength, we can upgrade the original supporting structure with heavier structural tubing when specified for the repair.

Hydraulic systems are another major part of our work. We repair and replace hydraulic cylinders, hoses, fittings, valves, reducers, pumps, electric motors, reservoirs, and PTO systems. Cylinders can be rebuilt with new seals and components when repairable, or completely replaced when necessary. We diagnose hydraulic leaks, damaged lines, bent cylinders, failed mounting points, and operating problems throughout the trailer.

Our electrical services include trailer wiring, complete rewiring, marker lights, rear lights, light boxes, connectors, switches, batteries, and electrical troubleshooting. We also service suspension and running gear, including shocks, brake chambers, drums, shoes, hubs, bearings, seals, wheels, and tires.

For older trailers and major restorations, we can take the project even further. The trailer can be completely stripped, sandblasted or laser cleaned, treated for corrosion, coated with industrial epoxy primer, and professionally painted with durable industrial or polyurethane coatings. We also perform targeted laser cleaning on hydraulic components, frames, and areas where precise corrosion removal is required.

We work on Cottrell, Sun Country, Sun Valley, Wally-Mo, Boydstun, Miller, LOHR systems, stinger units, enclosed car haulers, and other commercial auto transport equipment.

For Cottrell owners, Houston Car Hauler is a Cottrell Certified Warranty Repair Center, providing approved warranty and service work in Houston.

Our goal is not to cover problems with temporary patches. We identify the damaged area, restore the geometry, rebuild the structure, repair the mechanical and hydraulic systems, protect the trailer against corrosion, and return the equipment to reliable working condition.

From a hydraulic hose or cracked weld to a complete frame reconstruction and full trailer restoration, Car Hauler Repair Houston handles the entire process under one roof.
