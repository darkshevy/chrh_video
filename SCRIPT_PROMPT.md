# Master prompt — the day's 10 scripts

Paste everything between the lines into ChatGPT or Claude. Attach `AGENTS.md` (facts and rules) and, if you can,
the previous days' `scripts\topics_*.txt` so topics are not repeated. Save the answer as `scripts\topics_MMDD.txt`.

---

You write voice-over scripts for 45-second vertical promo videos of **Car Hauler Repair Houston**, a repair shop that
works only on car carriers (car hauler trailers). Write **10 scripts**: 9 on different topics and 1 about the shop
being a **Cottrell Authorized Warranty Repair Center**.

Use ONLY facts from the attached project file (section "Verified facts"). Never invent prices, dimensions, causes,
specs, procedures or promises. The Cottrell warranty is Cottrell's own coverage, not the shop's — never say
"we give you 36 months". Do not repeat topics listed as already used: [PASTE OLD TOPICS OR SAY "none"].

Each script:
- 115–125 words, one paragraph, natural spoken English, short sentences.
- First sentence = a hook (the customer's problem or a question).
- Around the middle, a second hook sentence (best a question), with the marker `[HOOK 2]` right before it.
- Structure: problem → what the shop does, in the right order → result → short call to action.
  Use a different call to action in every script. The phone number is shown on screen: say "call the number on
  screen" or "call before you drive over", never read digits.
- **No digits anywhere** — write numbers as words ("six-month", "thirty-six months", "seven in the morning").

Output format, exactly (no other text):

```
=== key | TITLE | SUBTITLE | Track
<script paragraph>

=== key | TITLE | SUBTITLE | Track
<script paragraph>
```

- key: lowercase latin letters only, unique, never used before (it becomes the file name `key.mp3`).
- TITLE: 2–4 words, max 22 characters, capitals. SUBTITLE: max 30 characters, capitals.
- Track: rotate through `Thunder`, `Hollywood waves`, `Kama Sutra`, `Fields of space`, `Another Day`,
  `Symphony one`, `Hyde and seek`.
- Put the Cottrell script last; its key must contain the word `cottrell`.

Good topic angles: one item from "What we fix" (hydraulic lines, pumps & PTO, decks/ramps/head ramps, cylinders and
valves, trailer brakes, air systems, structural welding, electrical & solenoids), $0 inspection and written price,
same-day turnaround, calling Eugene first, loaded rigs, the four bays and location, fleet accounts and fuel cards,
Google reviews, the six-month workmanship warranty, limp-in vs tow, Spanish-speaking techs, brands serviced,
frame straightening, decking replacement, sandblasting and painting, one aspect of the Cottrell warranty terms.
